Small by design

Enough can be very little.

01 / YOUR WEEK

Choose a gentle habit.

Set days per week and a minimum-effort version. One page of reading counts just as much as a longer session.

02 / A LITTLE SPACE

Focus on one thing.

Start a 1, 5, 10, 25 or 45 minute timer. Pause when you need to. Ending early never takes anything away.

03 / YOUR GARDEN

Keep something lovely.

Completed habit days and focus sessions add seeds. Unlock original garden illustrations that stay yours when you take a break.

No streak to protect.
No catch-up list waiting.

Come back when it helps. Use it less when life is flowing. Your garden is a place to return to, not another responsibility.

Your time. Your data.

The core works on your iPhone without an account. Cloud backup and usage counts are optional. Private feedback helps shape the app. The first garden stays free; an optional lifetime upgrade is being prepared.
