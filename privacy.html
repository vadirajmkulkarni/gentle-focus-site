Your garden.
Your data.

This policy describes the current prototype. The project is maintained by @vadirajmkulkarni. Contact us through in-app feedback or the support page.

What stays on your iPhone

Your optional name, selected intention, habits, up to one year of check-in dates, timer state, garden progress, settings and feedback drafts are saved in the app’s local storage. No sign-in is required for core features.

Optional cloud backup

If you enable backup, Firebase creates an anonymous identity and stores a copy of your app data in a private record reserved for this app. Access is restricted to that identity and the developer’s administrative access. Backup is tied to this installation: it is not a cross-device account, and reinstalling may lose access. Turning backup off stops new backups; use Delete My Data to remove existing records.

Feedback

Choosing Send Feedback sends the text and timestamp to the developer through Firebase, independently of the backup setting. Failed sends remain locally for retry. Please avoid personal, health or sensitive details. Feedback is not copied into usage analytics. No email address is requested, so direct replies are not available.

Optional usage counts

If you opt in, the app saves counts such as onboarding, completed habit days and completed focus sessions, app opens, session starts and early finishes. Counts can be included in your cloud backup. Habit names, your name and feedback text are not analytics events. Turn usage counts off to remove local saved counts; an updated backup is needed to replace previously backed-up counts.

Service providers and purchases

Google Firebase processes cloud identifiers, data you choose to send and service connection information. Optional purchases use Apple and RevenueCat, which process purchase records and technical customer identifiers to validate and restore purchases. There are no ads, advertising tracking, contacts, location, microphone or camera access.

Deletion and retention

You → Delete My Data removes this app’s local information, anonymous cloud identity, garden backup and sent feedback. Internet access is needed to delete cloud data; if deletion fails, the app retains local data and lets you retry. Cloud data otherwise remains until deletion. Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction records under their own policies and obligations; the app cannot erase Apple’s purchase history.

The website

This site is static and has no app-owned analytics, advertising tags or sign-up form. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process connection information under GitHub’s privacy policy.

Contact and changes

Use the in-app feedback button for private feedback, or our public support page. GitHub issues are public; do not include sensitive details. We will update this policy if data practices change.
